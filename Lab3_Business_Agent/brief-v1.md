# Tesla (TSLA) — CFO Business Briefing

## Audience
For a CFO evaluating equity exposure, business performance, and financial risk.

## Requested Window and Actual Price Observations
August 1–October 31, 2026. The dataset contains 64 daily observations, from August 3 through October 30; the requested endpoints fall on weekends.

## Prepared As Of
November 14, 2026, America/New_York; this is a retrospective briefing.

## Price Source
Yahoo Finance [P1], independently cross-checked against Nasdaq [P2]. All 64 closing prices match after consistent cent rounding.

## Price Basis
Unadjusted daily closing prices in USD, rounded half up to cents. This measures price appreciation, not total shareholder return.

## Price Performance
TSLA rose from **$322.08** to **$399.81**, an increase of **$77.73**, or **24.13%**. Python calculated `(399.81 / 322.08 - 1) × 100`. The [daily price chart](tesla-price-chart.png) plots every saved observation. Endpoint appreciation does not imply a smooth or risk-free holding period.

## Two Developments
1. **October 2:** Tesla reported Q3 production of **464,391 vehicles**, deliveries of **486,532 vehicles**, and **13.7 GWh** of energy storage deployments [N1]. CFO relevance: these operating volumes inform revenue and working-capital analysis, but deliveries and deployments alone cannot establish profitability or cash generation. Tesla explicitly cautions against treating them as quarterly financial results.
2. **October 21:** Tesla announced that its Q3 financial results were released on its investor-relations website [N2]. CFO relevance: the release is a checkpoint for assessing whether operating activity translated into earnings and cash flow. The advisory itself provides no financial figures; this briefing makes no claim about their level or direction.

## What It May Mean
**Interpretation:** The price gain warrants reviewing exposure limits and valuation assumptions. Reconcile operating volumes with margins, liquidity, and capital requirements before changing financial plans. Neither announcement is established here as a cause of the stock’s movement; this is not a buy-or-sell recommendation.

## Two Limitations
1. Closing prices omit intraday risk; no market benchmark or total-return comparison was performed.
2. Both developments are company-issued disclosures; the results advisory does not substantiate revenue, margin, or cash-flow conclusions.
